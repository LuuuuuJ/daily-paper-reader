## 摘要
实证研究在估计结构关系之前，通常会移除固定效应、潜在因子或高维控制变量。这些变换减少了混杂因素，但也可能移除了识别变异。我们研究了在双向依赖下，经过方程兼容的干扰项投影后的线性面板工具变量（IV）模型。投影后的雅可比矩阵决定了哪些结构方向保持可见；投影得分法则决定了它们的精度；在正态固定秩层上，它们结合成一个投影信息矩阵。我们推导了具有特定维度信息累积的弱识别极限、可行的因子转移条件、识别稳健性检验、针对非高斯交互极限的自助法程序，以及对投影谱、秩、子空间和信息矩阵的推断。仿真结果表明，原始的第一阶段统计量即使超过500也可能支持错误的符号，而投影诊断则揭示了有效的弱信息。在一项国际货币应用中，公共投影显著削弱了明显的国外产出持久性，而高斯参考的Anderson-Rubin集合仍然是无界的。因此，应在移除干扰项后评估识别情况。

## Abstract
Empirical work often removes fixed effects, latent factors, or high-dimensional controls before estimating structural relationships. These transformations reduce confounding but may also remove identifying variation. We study linear panel IV after one equation-compatible nuisance projection under two-way dependence. The projected Jacobian determines which structural directions remain visible; the projected-score law determines their precision; and, on Gaussian fixed-rank strata, they combine in a Projected Information Matrix. We derive weak-identification limits with dimension-specific information accumulation, feasible factor-transfer conditions, identification-robust tests, bootstrap procedures for non-Gaussian interaction limits, and inference for the projected spectrum, rank, subspaces, and information matrix. Simulations show that a raw first-stage statistic above 500 can support the wrong sign while projected diagnostics reveal weak valid information. In an international monetary application, common projection substantially attenuates apparent foreign-output persistence, while Gaussian-reference Anderson--Rubin sets remain unbounded. Identification should therefore be assessed after nuisance removal.
