## 摘要
我们研究了一类停止规则问题，其中 n 个家庭中的每一个都持续生育，直到其女孩数量首次等于其男孩数量的 d 倍加上一个正整数阈值 k_i。在所有家庭停止生育后，我们计算总人口中男孩的期望比例以及期望的男女孩比例。我们得到了这两个期望值的精确级数，并允许男孩和女孩的出生概率不等。期望比例公式还原了公平的首个女孩规则以及非加权女孩多数规则的已知结果。据我们所知，这些针对具有变动阈值的通用加权规则的求和公式是全新的。我们提供了两种初等证明，一种是双射证明，另一种是概率证明。

## Abstract
We study a class of stopping-rule questions in which each of \(n\) families continues having children until its number of girls first equals \(d\) times its number of boys plus a positive integer threshold \(k_i\). After all families have stopped, we ask for the expected proportion of boys and the expected boy-to-girl ratio in the combined population. We obtain exact series for both expectations, allowing the probabilities of a boy and a girl to be unequal. The expected-proportion formula recovers known results for the fair first-girl rule and for the unweighted girl-majority rule. To our knowledge, these summations for the general weighted rule with varying thresholds are new. We give two elementary proofs, one bijective and one probabilistic.
