## 摘要
本文研究了均值-方差框架下定额供款（DC）型养老金计划的投资与保险策略。我们考虑了一个具有时变利率、缴费和死亡风险的随机环境。DC 计划成员可以决定其债券和股票的配置，以及人寿保险的保额。采用鞅方法，我们推导出了闭式最优策略和均值-方差有效前沿。进一步的数值分析探讨了死亡率改善如何影响投资和保险决策，以及最优决策对市场参数的敏感性。我们的分析表明，长寿提高了对未来缴费的预期，使得养老金成员能够采取风险较低的投资策略。同时，保险策略向成年早期转移，以保护高价值的未来收入，并在高龄阶段显著下降。此外，我们对目标预期财富、风险市场价格和缴费增长进行了敏感性分析。这些研究结果为养老金成员的投资提供了实践指导，并为 DC 型养老金计划的设计提供了见解。

## Abstract
This paper studies the investment and insurance strategies of defined-contribution (DC) pension plans under the mean-variance framework. We consider a stochastic environment with time-varying interest rates, contributions, and mortality risk. The DC plan members are allowed to decide their bond and stock allocations, as well as their life insurance coverage. Adopting the martingale approach, we derive the closed-form optimal strategies and the mean-variance efficient frontier. Further numerical analysis investigates how mortality improvements affect investment and insurance decisions, as well as the sensitivity of the optimal decision to market parameters. Our analysis suggests that longevity raises expectations of future contributions, allowing pension members to adopt a less risky investment strategy. Meanwhile, insurance strategy shifts toward early adulthood to protect the high value of future income and decreases significantly at later ages. Moreover, we conduct sensitivity analyses on the target expected wealth, market price of risk, and contribution growth. These findings provide practical guidance for pension members on investment and offer insights for the design of DC pension plans.
