## 摘要
妊娠期用药研究有两个主要目标：一是检测药物可能对孕妇或胎儿有害的信号，二是为需要药物治疗的孕妇提供更好的治疗支持。目标试验模拟（Target trial emulation）已被提出作为一种利用真实世界数据评估干预效果的方法，近期已扩展到妊娠领域。该方法侧重于使入组资格和治疗启动与随访开始保持一致，鉴于妊娠期特有的方法学挑战（如左右删失与截断、竞争事件、孕周差异以及不同的病因敏感期），这一点尤为重要。虽然以往关于妊娠期目标试验模拟的研究主要集中在疫苗或抗生素等单点治疗的启动与否，但本文侧重于有关孕前治疗方案变更的研究问题，旨在强调设计与临床实践中妊娠早期治疗决策相关时间点相一致的研究机会和方法。本文以 2 型糖尿病的治疗为例，回顾了在常规收集的医疗保健数据中识别妊娠事件的方法，介绍了可能的“零时刻”（time zero）候选方案，并讨论了旨在最大限度减少因选择偏倚和不朽人时（immortal person time）引起潜在偏倚的分析方法。

## Abstract
Research on the use of medications during pregnancy has two primary goals: to detect signals that medications may be harmful to a pregnant individual or fetus, and to support better treatment of pregnant people who require pharmacotherapy. Target trial emulation has been proposed as an approach to estimate the effects of interventions in real world data, with recent extensions to the pregnancy setting. This approach focuses on aligning eligibility and treatment initiation with start of follow up, which is particularly desirable given methodological challenges specific to pregnancy, such as right and left censoring and truncation, competing events, differences in gestational length, and varying etiologically susceptible periods. While previous work on target trial emulation in pregnancy has focused on initiation versus non-initiation of point treatments such as vaccines or antibiotics, this paper focuses on research questions regarding changes to pregestational treatment regimes, and aims to highlight opportunities and approaches to designing studies that align with relevant time points during early pregnancy at which treatment decisions occur in clinical practice. Using the example of treatment for type 2 diabetes mellitus, we review methods for identifying pregnancy episodes in routinely collected healthcare data, introduce possible time zero candidates, and discuss analytic approaches that minimize potential bias due to selection and immortal person time.
