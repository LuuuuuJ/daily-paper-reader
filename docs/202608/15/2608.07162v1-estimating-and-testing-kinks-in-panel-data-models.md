## 摘要
许多经济和金融关系可能是逐渐变化而非突变的。我们研究了面板数据模型，其中系数向量在日历时间内是连续且分段线性的，并具有有限个斜率发生变化的未知拐点日期。我们提出了一种惩罚最小二乘估计量，该估计量对系数路径的二阶差分应用自适应加权组惩罚，并建立了渐近理论，证明其能以趋于1的概率恢复拐点的数量和位置。据我们所知，这是首个在固定效应下估计时变系数路径中未知数量公共拐点日期的面板框架。我们证明了端点斜率以通常的三次方制度长度速率收敛，而内部斜率的收敛速率由其自身及相邻制度的长度决定。我们还开发了一个逐系数扩展模型，允许单个回归变量在不同日期发生拐点。蒙特卡洛证据支持了良好的有限样本性质，我们通过宏观金融领域的一个应用（具体为债务与增长之间的关系）展示了该方法。

## Abstract
Many economic and financial relationships may change gradually rather than abruptly. We study panel data models in which the coefficient vector is continuous and piecewise linear in calendar time, with a finite number of unknown kink dates at which its slope changes. We propose a penalised least squares estimator that applies adaptive weighted group penalties to the second differences of the coefficient path, and develop asymptotic theory showing that it recovers both the number and the locations of the kinks with probability approaching one. To our knowledge, this is the first panel framework to estimate an unknown number of common kink dates in a time-varying coefficient path under fixed effects. We establish that endpoint slopes converge at the usual cubic regime-length rate and interior slopes at rates determined by their own and adjacent regime lengths. We also develop a coefficient-by-coefficient extension allowing individual regressors to kink at different dates. Monte Carlo evidence supports the good finite sample properties, and we illustrate the method through an application in macro-finance, specifically the relationship between debt and growth.
