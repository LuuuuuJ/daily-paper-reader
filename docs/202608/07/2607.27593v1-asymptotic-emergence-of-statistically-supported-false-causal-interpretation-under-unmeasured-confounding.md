## 摘要
未观测到的混杂因素被广泛认为是观测因果推断的一个局限，但其对数据驱动的因果发现的影响往往被低估。我们对这一局限性进行了渐近表征。当真实的因果变量未被观测到，但存在相关的代理变量时，在寻找日益稳定的数据驱动因果结构的过程中增加样本量，会使涉及可观测代理变量的关联变得越来越确定。这一结果突显了减少统计不确定性与恢复因果机制之间的根本区别：更多的数据可以提高对观测变量空间内真实关系的确定性，但无法保证识别出底层的因果结构。

## Abstract
Unmeasured confounding is widely recognized as a limitation of observational causal inference, but its implications for data-driven causal discovery are often understated. We provide an asymptotic characterization of this limitation. When the true causal variable is unobserved but correlated proxy variables are available, increasing sample size while searching for increasingly stable, data-driven causal structures will make associations involving observable proxies become increasingly certain. This result highlights a fundamental distinction between reducing statistical uncertainty and recovering causal mechanisms: more data can improve certainty about genuine relationships within the observed variable space while providing no guarantee of identifying the underlying causal structure.
