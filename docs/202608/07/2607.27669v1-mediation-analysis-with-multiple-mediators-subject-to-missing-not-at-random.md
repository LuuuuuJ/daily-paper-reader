## 摘要
因果中介分析是揭示连接处理与结果的中介机制的关键工具。现有的多重中介分析方法通常假设观测数据完整或为随机缺失，当中介变量值是非随机缺失（MNAR）时，可能会产生有偏估计。本文研究了在存在非随机缺失的情况下，具有多个中介变量的因果中介效应的识别与估计。我们考虑了一类广泛的非随机缺失机制，其中缺失可能取决于未观测到的中介变量、处理、协变量和结果。在一系列日益通用的非随机缺失机制下，我们确立了可识别的自然直接效应和间接效应，有效地将现有的中介分析推广到处理不可忽略的缺失中介变量的情况。基于所提出的识别框架，我们开发了因果中介效应的估计程序，并通过模拟研究评估了它们的有限样本性能。结果表明，在各种缺失场景下，该方法均表现出令人满意的性能。对美国国家健康与营养调查（NHANES）数据的应用说明了所提方法在存在不可忽略缺失数据的情况下研究中介路径的实际效用。

## Abstract
Causal mediation analysis serves as a key tool for uncovering the mediating mechanisms linking treatments to outcomes. Existing methods for mediation analysis with multiple mediators typically assume complete observations or missing-at-random and may yield biased estimation when mediator values are missing not at random (MNAR). This paper studies the identification and estimation of causal mediation effects with multiple mediators subject to MNAR missingness. We consider a broad class of MNAR mechanisms in which missingness may depend on unobserved mediators, treatment, covariates, and outcomes. Under a series of increasingly general MNAR mechanisms, we establish identified natural direct and indirect effects, effectively generalizing existing mediation analysis to handle nonignorable missing mediators. Based on the proposed identification framework, we develop estimation procedures for causal mediation effects and evaluate their finite-sample performance through simulation studies. The results demonstrate satisfactory performance across a range of missingness scenarios. An application to data from the National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey(NHANES) illustrates the practical utility of the proposed methodology for investigating mediation pathways in the presence of nonignorable missing data.
