## 摘要
中介分析研究处理效应的一部分是否通过处理与主要结局之间因果路径上的一个或多个中介变量进行传递。然而，存在多个且可能相互依赖的中介变量带来了巨大挑战，特别是当结局为二分类、中介变量在不同尺度上测量且存在交互作用时。我们考虑了具有二分类处理和二分类结局的因果中介分析，在相对风险尺度上定义了自然直接效应、间接效应和总效应，从而避免了与非可折叠效应测量相关的解释困难。在顺序可忽略性假设下，我们开发了一个统一的基于回归的框架，可容纳多个连续型、二分类或混合型中介变量。所提出的框架考虑了中介变量之间的依赖关系，并允许暴露-中介以及中介-中介之间的交互作用。我们推导了不同中介变量设置下因果效应的闭式表达式，并开发了一种基于似然的推理程序，用于估计因果效应并量化其不确定性。该方法通过两个实证应用进行了说明。

## Abstract
Mediation analysis investigates whether part of the treatment effect is channelled through one or more mediators along the causal pathway between the treatment and the primary outcome. However, the presence of multiple, potentially dependent mediators raises substantial challenges, particularly when the outcome is binary, the mediators are measured on different scales, and interactions are present. We consider on causal mediation analysis with a binary treatment and a binary outcome, defining natural direct, indirect, and total effects on the relative-risk scale, thereby avoiding the interpretational difficulties associated with non-collapsible effect measures. Under a sequential ignorability assumption, we develop a unified regression-based framework that accommodates multiple continuous, binary, or mixed mediators. The proposed framework accounts for dependence among mediators and allows for both exposure-mediator and mediator-mediator interactions. We derive closed-form expressions for the causal effects across the different mediator settings and develop a likelihood-based inference procedure for estimating the causal effects and quantifying their uncertainty. The methodology is illustrated through two empirical applications.
