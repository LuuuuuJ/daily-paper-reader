## 摘要
对来自多项研究的自然间接效应估计值进行元分析，正越来越多地被用于综合感兴趣的因果路径证据。然而，标准的中介元分析方法通常基于结构方程模型，这无法解释中介变量与结局之间的混杂因素，不易扩展以处理缺失的中介变量和结局数据，且通常不明确汇总间接效应所适用的目标人群。在这项工作中，我们提出了一种解决这些局限性的新方法。我们的方法在证据合成之前，将特定研究的自然间接效应估计值迁移到一个定义明确的目标人群。所提出的方法能够整合那些没有明确研究中介作用但收集了中介变量数据的研究，以提高广泛性。利用半参数理论，我们为目标参数构建了灵活的、数据自适应的估计量。还开发了基于方差分析（ANOVA）离差平方和的新型随机效应模型及其非参数模拟，以将研究间的异质性分解为可能影响所得结论因果可解释性的不同来源。通过模拟和真实世界数据评估了所提方法的有限样本性能。

## Abstract
Meta-analyzing natural indirect effect estimates from multiple studies is increas- ingly used to synthesize evidence on causal pathways of interest. However, stan- dard mediation meta-analysis approaches are typically based on structural equation modeling, which fails to account for mediator-outcome confounding, is not read- ily extended to address missing mediator and outcome data, and is often unclear about the target population to which the summary indirect effect pertains. In this work, we propose a novel method that addresses these limitations. Our ap- proach transports study-specific natural indirect effect estimates to a well-defined target population prior to evidence synthesis. The proposed methods enable the integration of studies that do not explicitly investigate mediation but collect data on the mediator to improve extensiveness. Using semiparametric theory, we con- struct flexible, data-adaptive estimators for the target parameter. Novel random- effects models and non-parametric analogues based on ANOVA sums of squares are also developed to decompose between-study heterogeneity into distinct sources that may affect the causal interpretability of the obtained findings. Finite-sample per- formance of the proposed methods is evaluated through simulated and real-world data.
