## 摘要
受 Brand & Xie (2010) 关于教育异质性回报研究的启发（该研究探讨了完成大学学业对收入的影响如何随完成大学的未知概率而变化），我们分析了在第一步中估计某些协变量时，估计非参数回归函数的问题。将估计的协变量视为已知量的插入式估计量（Plug-in estimators）通常会受到第一阶段估计误差的影响。为了缓解这一问题，我们在一个与第一阶段估计方法选择无关的框架内分析了两种去偏方法，该框架在第二阶段回归中依赖于局部平滑或基于筛分（sieve-based）的方法。具体而言，我们考虑了：(i) 基于影响函数的路径可微参数估计量，用于逼近目标被估量；(ii) 一种旨在直接修正其偏差的插入式估计量变体。对于每种方法，我们给出了估计误差的上界，并刻画了接近 Oracle 速率的条件，强调了相对于插入式估计量在收敛速度方面可能获得的提升。仿真研究展示了这些方法的有限样本行为。我们将该方法应用于 1997 年美国国家青年纵向调查（NLSY97）的数据，并发现证据表明，对于完成大学概率最低的个体，完成大学学业对失业率的降低作用最大，这与文献中的早期发现（Brand & Xie 2010; Brand 2023）一致。

## Abstract
Motivated by the study of heterogeneous returns to education in Brand & Xie 2010, which considers how the effect of completing college on earnings varies with the (unknown) probability of completing college, we analyze the problem of estimating a nonparametric regression function when certain covariates are estimated in a first step. Plug-in estimators that treat the estimated covariates as known generally suffer from first-stage estimation error. To mitigate this issue, we analyze two debiasing approaches within a framework that is agnostic to the choice of the first-stage estimation method and relies on either local-smoothing or sieve-based methods for the second-stage regression. In particular, we consider: (i) influence function-based estimators of pathwise differentiable parameters that approximate the target estimand, and (ii) a variant of plug-in estimators that directly aims to correct their bias. For each method, we upper bound the estimation error and characterize conditions under which oracle rates can be approached, highlighting the possible gains in terms of convergence rates relative to the plug-ins. Simulation studies illustrate the finite-sample behavior of the methods. We apply our methodology to data from the National Longitudinal Survey of Youth 1997 and find evidence that completing college yields the largest reductions in unemployment for individuals least likely to do so, consistent with earlier findings in the literature (Brand & Xie 2010; Brand 2023).
