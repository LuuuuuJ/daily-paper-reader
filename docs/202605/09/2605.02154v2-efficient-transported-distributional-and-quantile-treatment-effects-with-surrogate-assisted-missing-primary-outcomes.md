## 摘要
我们研究了目标总体的分布和分位数处理效应。在这种情况下，源研究观察了所有源单位的处理和处理后代理变量，但仅对验证子集观察了长期主要结局，而目标总体仅提供基线协变量。目标待估参数是迁移的反事实分布函数 $ψ_a(y)=P(Y^a\le y\mid R=0)$、它们的分位数 $q_a(τ)$ 以及分位数处理效应 $Δ(τ)=q_1(τ)-q_0(τ)$。代理变量不被视为替代终点，也不施加 Prentice 型代理条件。相反，代理变量仅用于在随机缺失的主要结局采样下提高效率。我们推导了非参数有效影响函数，它具有三个正交分量，分别对应于目标协变量采样、源代理过程和缺失的主要结局。这在干扰参数估计后产生了一个闭式交叉拟合一步估计量。我们建立了识别性、正则梯度、精确漂移恒等式、比率级稳健性、迁移 CDF 的点向和一致渐近线性、显式局部逆映射条件下的分位数 Bahadur 表示、显式估计过程和密度条件下的高层乘法自助法同时置信带，以及观察代理变量带来的分位数特定效率增益。我们还针对一类特意限制的可分析有界有限维或有限秩实现，给出了低层干扰项速率验证，这些实现基于筛分岭回归、岭逻辑回归、校准密度比估计、有限秩核岭回归以及显式网格、特征值、源和熵条件下的保序投影。

## Abstract
We study target-population distributional and quantile treatment effects when a source study observes treatment and post-treatment surrogates for all source units but observes a long-run primary outcome only for a validation subset, while the target population contributes only baseline covariates. The target estimands are transported counterfactual distribution functions $ψ_a(y)=P(Y^a\le y\mid R=0)$, their quantiles $q_a(τ)$, and the quantile treatment effect $Δ(τ)=q_1(τ)-q_0(τ)$. The surrogate is not treated as a replacement endpoint and no Prentice-type surrogacy condition is imposed. Instead, the surrogate is used only to improve efficiency under missing-at-random primary-outcome sampling. We derive the nonparametric efficient influence function, which has three orthogonal components corresponding to target covariate sampling, the source surrogate process, and missing primary outcomes. This yields a closed-form cross-fitted one-step estimator after nuisance estimation. We establish identification, the canonical gradient, exact drift identities, ratio-level robustness, pointwise and uniform asymptotic linearity for transported CDFs, Bahadur representations for quantiles under explicit local inverse-map conditions, high-level multiplier-bootstrap simultaneous bands under explicit estimated-process and density conditions, and quantile-specific efficiency gains from observing surrogates. We also give lower-level nuisance-rate verification for a deliberately restricted class of analyzable bounded finite-dimensional or finite-rank implementations based on sieve ridge regression, ridge logistic regression, calibrated density-ratio estimation, finite-rank kernel ridge regression, and isotonic projection under explicit grid, eigenvalue, source, and entropy conditions.
