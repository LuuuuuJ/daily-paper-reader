## 摘要
计数数据模型在许多领域无处不在，然而此类模型的贝叶斯数据增强算法经常面临马尔可夫链蒙特卡罗（MCMC）效率方面的挑战。当对具有高比例零值结果的数据进行建模时，后验模拟尤其具有挑战性。在本文中，我们通过为半参数贝叶斯计数数据回归模型引入一种边缘数据增强方法来解决这一问题，该方法基于一个对零计数观测值对应的潜在结果进行重新缩放的工作参数。这一策略缓解了通常会降低标准数据增强方案效率的强后验依赖性，并显著提高了采样效率。合成数据示例和仿真研究被用于证明与传统采样方法相比在混合性能上的改进。一项利用潜因子分析对奥地利地方死亡人数进行建模的人口学应用，进一步强调了所提方法的广泛适用性。

## Abstract
Count data models are ubiquitous in many fields, yet Bayesian data augmentation algorithms for such models frequently encounter challenges with Markov chain Monte Carlo efficiency. Posterior simulation is especially demanding when modeling data with a high proportion of zero outcomes. In this paper, we address this issue by introducing a marginal data augmentation approach for semi-parametric Bayesian count data regression models, based on a working parameter that rescales latent outcomes corresponding to zero-count observations. This strategy alleviates the strong posterior dependencies that typically reduce the efficiency of standard data augmentation schemes and leads to substantial gains in sampling efficiency. Synthetic data examples and simulation studies are used to demonstrate the improvements in mixing compared to conventional sampling methods. A demographic application using latent factor analysis to model subnational mortality counts in Austria further underscores the broader applicability of the proposed methodology.
