## 摘要
传统的异方差性诊断关注回归扰动项的条件方差是否随协变量变化。本文提出了一个不同的问题：这种变化何时会影响对目标估计量的推断？本文开发了一种针对特定对比（contrast-specific）的理论，用于刻画协方差扰动在何时具有推断相关性。我们证明，对于线性回归中的任何线性对比 $a'β$，异方差稳健方差与池化固定设计方差之间的差异，取决于条件误差方差与特定对比杠杆得分（leverage score）之间的经验协方差。因此，模型中可能存在异方差性，但对于特定的系数或线性组合而言，它在阶数上是无关的。相反，如果适度的异方差性集中在对目标对比具有高度信息量的观测值上，则可能产生巨大的推断影响。我们通过异方差相关比率和标准误膨胀因子精确地刻画了这种影响，将结果与成对自助法（pairs bootstrap）和残差自助法（residual bootstrap）程序联系起来，并将该分解扩展到一般的协方差结构，其中非对角线依赖项贡献了一个独立的特定对比项。研究结果提供了一种统一的方法，用以理解为什么在同一回归中，稳健标准误、聚类标准误和自助法标准误在不同系数之间会存在差异。

## Abstract
Conventional heteroskedasticity diagnostics ask whether the conditional variance of the regression disturbance varies with covariates. This paper asks a different question: when does that variation matter for inference on the estimand of interest? The paper develops a contrast-specific theory characterizing when covariance perturbations are inferentially relevant. We show that, for any linear contrast $a'β$ in a linear regression, the difference between the heteroskedasticity-robust variance and the pooled fixed-design variance is governed by the empirical covariance between conditional error variance and a contrast-specific leverage score. Thus, heteroskedasticity may be present in the model yet first-order irrelevant for a particular coefficient or linear combination. Conversely, modest heteroskedasticity may have a large inferential effect if it is concentrated on observations that are highly informative for the contrast of interest. We characterize the effect exactly through a heteroskedasticity relevance ratio and a standard-error inflation factor, relate the result to pairs and residual bootstrap procedures, and extend the decomposition to general covariance structures, where off-diagonal dependence contributes a separate contrast-specific term. The results provide a unified way to understand why robust, clustered, and bootstrap standard errors can differ across coefficients in the same regression.
