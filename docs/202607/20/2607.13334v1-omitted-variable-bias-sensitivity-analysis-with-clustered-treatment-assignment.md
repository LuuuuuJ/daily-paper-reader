## 摘要
Cinelli and Hazlett (2020) 为线性回归模型开发了一种敏感性分析方法，该方法通过两个偏 $R^2$ 参数对遗漏变量偏差进行参数化，这两个参数分别捕捉了遗漏混杂因素在处理和结果中解释的残差变异。当处理是在更高聚合层级分配时（如在聚类观察设计中），该方法通常应用于拟合单位级数据的回归。本文表明，尽管单位级回归与适当加权的聚类聚合回归在估计处理效应方面具有数值等效性，但敏感性分析程序会根据所选的分析层级得出不同的结论。结果-混杂因素偏 $R^2$ 同时反映了组间和组内变异，但后者与遗漏变量偏差无关，因为从结构上讲，组内变异无法由组级混杂因素解释。通过使用 Pearson 偏 $\eta$ 对单位级回归的稳健性值和极端情景分析进行直接修正，可以恢复这两种方法之间的等效性。本文最后对以单位级协变量为基准提出了警示，并建议始终将这些协变量的聚类级平均值作为回归变量包含在内 (Mundlak, 1978)。

## Abstract
Cinelli and Hazlett (2020) develops a sensitivity analysis method for the linear regression model that parameterizes omitted variable bias in terms of two partial $R^2$ parameters capturing the residual variation explained by an omitted confounder in the treatment and outcome respectively. This method is often applied to regressions fit to unit-level data when treatment is assigned at a higher level of aggregation -- as in clustered observational designs. This paper shows that despite the numerical equivalence of the unit-level regression and an appropriately weighted cluster-aggregated regression for estimating the treatment effect, the sensitivity analysis procedure yields different conclusions depending on the chosen level of analysis. The outcome-confounder partial $R^2$ reflects both between- and within- group variation but the latter is irrelevant to omitted variable bias as it by construction cannot be explained by a group-level confounder. Straightforward corrections to the robustness value and the extreme scenario analysis from the unit-level regression using Pearson's partial-$η$ recover equivalence between these two approaches. The paper concludes with a point of caution when benchmarking against unit-level covariates and recommends always including cluster-level averages of these covariates as regressors (Mundlak, 1978).
