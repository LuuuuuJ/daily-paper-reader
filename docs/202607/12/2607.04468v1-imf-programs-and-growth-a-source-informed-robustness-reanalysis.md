## 摘要
本文重新评估了关于国际货币基金组织（IMF）计划对经济增长影响的元分析证据。研究起点是 Balima 和 Sokolova 具有影响力的元分析，该研究汇集了来自 36 项研究的 994 个估计值，并报告了具有显著异质性的正向平均效应。本文提出的再分析提出了四个更严格的要求：它将研究（而非报告的估计值）视为主要的推断单位；使用基于来源信息的因果可信度分类；通过研究聚合、相关效应敏感性、多层 CR2 推断和稳健方差估计来模拟研究内相关性；并通过 Egger、PET、PEESE、类 WAAP 高精度分析、p-curve 诊断、剪补法和探索性选择模型评估发表偏倚敏感性。核心结论并非 IMF 计划在各地都会降低增长，也不是真实效应恰好为零。结论更为具体且有力：一旦将相关性、发表选择、异质性和识别的可信度视为首要问题，总体文献中的正向平均效应就不再稳健。在最合理的模型设定中，平均效应在统计上与零无异，而与实质上微不足道效应的等价性仅得到部分支持，且取决于所选的等价边界。

## Abstract
This article reassesses the meta-analytic evidence on the effect of International Monetary Fund programs on economic growth. The point of departure is the influential meta-analysis by Balima and Sokolova, which assembles 994 estimates from 36 studies and reports a positive average effect with substantial heterogeneity. The reanalysis presented here imposes four stricter requirements. It treats the study, rather than the reported estimate, as the primary inferential unit; it uses a source-informed classification of causal credibility; it models within-study dependence through study aggregation, correlated-effects sensitivity, multilevel CR2 inference, and robust variance estimation; and it evaluates publication-bias sensitivity through Egger, PET, PEESE, WAAP-like top-precision analysis, p-curve diagnostics, trim-and-fill, and exploratory selection models. The central result is not that IMF programs reduce growth everywhere, nor that the true effect is exactly zero. The result is narrower and stronger: the positive average effect in the aggregate literature is not robust once dependence, publication selection, heterogeneity, and credibility of identification are treated as first-order concerns. In the most defensible specifications, the average effect is statistically indistinguishable from zero, while equivalence to a substantively negligible effect is only partially supported and depends on the chosen equivalence bound.
