## 摘要
营销系统经常经历可能影响绩效的运营变更，因此及时检测不利影响对于决策至关重要。虽然贝叶斯因果影响（Bayesian Causal Impact）模型被广泛用于评估干预措施的因果效应，但其支持早期运营监控的能力仍有待进一步探索。本文提出了一个基于模拟的框架，用于评估在受控性能退化下贝叶斯因果影响分析的灵敏度和检测能力。利用来自“弃购挽回”营销路径的每日流量数据，我们反复扰动观察结果，并评估在不同效应量、置信水平和检测周期下的警报激活概率。本文分析了两种警报准则：一种基于观测值低于预测下限的比例，另一种基于负累积影响的连续天数。结果表明，检测性能在很大程度上取决于效应大小、置信水平和评估周期之间的相互作用。特别是，随着监控周期的增加，基于比例的准则变得不那么有效，而基于持续性的准则提供了更稳定且在运营上更有意义的检测行为。所提出的框架将因果影响分析扩展到了回顾性效应估计之外，为量化检测灵敏度并支持动态营销环境中的监控决策提供了一种实用的方法论。

## Abstract
Marketing systems frequently undergo operational changes that may affect performance, making timely detection of adverse effects essential for decision making. While Bayesian Causal Impact models are widely used to estimate the causal effects of interventions, their ability to support early operational monitoring remains less explored. This paper proposes a simulation based framework to evaluate the sensitivity and detection capabilities of Bayesian causal impact analysis under controlled performance degradations. Using daily traffic data from an abandoned cart marketing journey, we repeatedly perturb the observed outcomes and assess alarm activation probabilities across different effect magnitudes, confidence levels, and detection horizons. Two alarm criteria are analyzed: one based on the proportion of observations falling below the predictive lower bound and another based on consecutive days of negative cumulative impact. Results show that detection performance depends strongly on the interaction between effect size, confidence level, and evaluation horizon. In particular, proportion based criteria become less effective as the monitoring horizon increases, whereas persistence based criteria provide more stable and operationally meaningful detection behavior. The proposed framework extends causal impact analysis beyond retrospective effect estimation, offering a practical methodology for quantifying detection sensitivity and supporting monitoring decisions in dynamic marketing environments.
